## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Inkpot is a simple diary for home tuition teachers: your fee register, attendance and parent reminders in one place. Add your batches and students once, and every month the app works out who owes what — so you always know who has paid, who paid half, and who is behind.

No signup. No internet needed. Your register stays on your phone.

WHAT IT DOES

• Monthly bills, made for you
Every student is billed again on the 1st of each month. You never enter students again.

• Students who join mid-month
A student joining on the 18th? Choose a part-month bill now, add it to next month, or bill from the 18th every month. The app shows the parent's exact amounts before you decide.

• Full and part payments
Record what a parent actually paid. Part payments are carried forward, and advance payments are used on the next bill automatically.

• Receipts in one tap
Send a neat receipt image to the parent on WhatsApp after every payment.

• WhatsApp reminders
Remind parents with a polite message already written in Roman Urdu. WhatsApp opens with the message ready — you still press send, one parent at a time.

• Attendance in seconds
Tap only the students who did not come. Everyone else counts as present. Optionally tell the parent on WhatsApp.

• Leaving students
When a student leaves mid-month, the app settles the bill for the days actually taught, and tells you whether money is still to collect or to give back.

• Reminders for you
Optional notifications when a batch starts and on the morning a fee is due. Off until you switch them on.

• Backup and restore
Save your whole register to a file, and restore it on a new phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Inkpot has no account and no server. It does not use the internet, show ads or track you. Your students' details never leave your phone unless you share a receipt, send a WhatsApp message or export a backup yourself.

WHO IT IS FOR

Teachers who run tuition at home, with a few batches and 10 to 50 students, who are tired of keeping fees in a notebook or in their head.
```

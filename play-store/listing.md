## Full description (4000 characters max)

3,125 / 4,000

```
Inkpot is a teacher's diary for home tuition: your fee register, attendance register and parent reminders in one simple app. Add your batches and students once, and every month Inkpot works out who owes what, so you always know who has paid, who paid half and who is behind.

Made for home tuition teachers in Pakistan who teach a few batches and keep fees in a notebook or in their head. Amounts are in rupees, and messages to parents are written in Roman Urdu. No signup and no internet needed: Inkpot works offline, and your whole fees ka hisaab stays on your phone.

WHAT INKPOT DOES

• Monthly bills, made for you
Every month Inkpot makes each student's new bill automatically, so your fees record stays up to date. You never enter students again.

• Students who join mid-month
A student joins on the 18th? Pick one of three ways to bill: a part-month bill now, the part month added to next month's bill, or a bill on the 18th of every month. Inkpot shows the exact amounts for each choice, so you can read them out to the parent before you decide.

• Full, part and advance payments
Record what the parent actually paid. Whatever is left is carried forward, the oldest bill is paid first, and an advance is used on the next bill automatically.

• This month at a glance
See the total still pending, how much of this month's tuition fees you have collected, and who has paid and who has not. Use the arrows to look back at earlier months.

• Fee receipts
After a payment, share a neat receipt image with the parent on WhatsApp or another app.

• Fee reminders on WhatsApp
Inkpot opens WhatsApp with a polite Roman Urdu reminder already written, for example: "Assalam o Alaikum. Ahmed ki October 2026 ki fees Rs 3,000 abhi baqi hai. Meherbani farma kar jama karwa dein. Shukriya." You read it and press send yourself, one parent at a time. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

• Attendance in seconds
Tap only the students who did not come; everyone else counts as present. If a parent's number is saved, Inkpot can open a short WhatsApp note to them for you to send.

• Editing and leaving students
Change a student's name, the parent's number or the fee. When a student leaves mid-month, Inkpot settles the bill for the days actually taught and tells you whether money is still to collect or to give back.

• Reminders for you
Optional notifications when a batch starts, so you remember to mark attendance, and on the morning a fee is due. They stay off until you switch them on.

• Backup and restore
Save your whole fee register to a backup file, and restore it on a new phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Inkpot has no account and no server, and it does not use the internet. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your fees record and your students' details never leave your phone unless you share a receipt, send a WhatsApp message or export a backup yourself.

WHO IT IS FOR

Home tuition teachers with a few batches, who are tired of keeping fees and attendance in a notebook and working out every month who still has to pay. The app is in English; reminders and receipt messages for parents are in Roman Urdu.
```

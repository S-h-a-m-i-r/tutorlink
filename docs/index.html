Inkpot

Know who has paid. Always.

The teacher's diary for home tuition — your fee register, attendance and parent reminders on your phone.

What it does

Monthly bills, made for you

Every student is billed again each month. You never enter students twice.

Full and part payments

Record what a parent actually paid. What's left is carried forward.

Receipts and reminders

Send receipts and polite Roman Urdu reminders on WhatsApp. You press send.

Attendance in seconds

Tap only the students who did not come. Everyone else is present.

Mid-month joining

See the parent's exact bill for each option before you decide.

Backup and restore

Save your register to a file and bring it to a new phone.

Private by design

No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Inkpot does not use the internet. Your students' details stay on your phone unless you share them yourself. Read the privacy policy.
